INDICATE LIFE · For the national teams

Reporting your data with the INDICATE template

The national datasets were delivered before the INDICATE reporting template was finalised, and nobody was asked to use it. The dashboard therefore harmonises each dataset to the template itself. This page helps you check that harmonisation and, where you can, complete your data in the template format. It is a working page for your team, not an assessment of the datasets.

How to read it
